Framer has established itself as one of the most powerful design-to-web tools in the industry. It gives designers print-like layout control, combined with native production-ready animations. The best part? You can use nearly all of these features completely for free. 

Framer's **Free Plan** is surprisingly generous. It allows you to build full, complex, multi-page websites without inputting a credit card. However, the moment you want to point your site to a custom domain (like `mybusiness.com`), Framer prompts you to upgrade to a paid hosting subscription.

In this guide, we will look at what you can do with Framer's free plan, what you lose, and how you can work around the custom domain limit for just $3/month.

---

## What Framer's free plan includes

Framer's free tier is not a simple limited trial; it is a fully functional design platform. Here is what is included:

- **Full Editor Access:** Use all canvas, design, styling, positioning, and animation tools.
- **Components & Interactions:** Build reusable design components, add hover states, trigger scroll-based animations, and create complex navigation menus.
- **Publish to a Subdomain:** Publish your site instantly to a free `.framer.website` (or `.framer.app`, `.framer.media`) subdomain.
- **Unlimited Projects:** Create as many test sites or visual drafts as you want.
- **AI Design Tools:** Generate design layouts and text copies directly inside the editor using AI assistants.

For most designers and builders, this package provides everything needed to construct a professional, high-performance website.

---

## What you miss on the free plan

If the free plan is so robust, why does anyone upgrade? There are only two major limitations on the free tier:

1. **No Custom Domain:** You cannot point your site to `yourdomain.com`. You are forced to use their standard `.framer.website` subdomain.
2. **The Framer Badge:** All free sites feature a small "Made in Framer" badge floating in the bottom-right corner of the page.

To remove this badge and connect a custom domain natively, Framer requires you to upgrade to the **Basic** plan, which costs **$10 to $15 per site, every month**. For simple landing pages or side projects, this is a steep price.
